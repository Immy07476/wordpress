Welcome to Uganda Martyrs University

This university is found in Nkozi, Mpigi District, in the Central Region of Uganda, along the Kampala-Masaka highway.

It is located on Nkozi Hills approximately 82 to 85 kilometres southwest of Kampala, near the equator and about 2 kilometres off the main highway at Kayabwe.

We warmly welcome all the new students to this fantastic project at Uganda Martyrs University.

This project was started by a group of creative and innovative students on 26 August 2022. It has positively impacted students who have joined it.
