HISTORY OF UGANDA MARTYRS UNIVERSITY

Uganda Martyrs University (UMU) is a private, faith-based university established in 1993 by the Uganda Episcopal Conference. Its main campus is located at Nkozi in Uganda. The university is committed to providing quality higher education, research and community service.

OUR VISION

To be a nationally and internationally recognised university for excellence in teaching, learning, research, advancement of knowledge and community engagement.

OUR MISSION

To provide quality higher education, training and research for the betterment of society, guided by ethical values.

OUR CORE VALUES

  • Transparency
  • Accountability
  • Reliability
  • Integrity
  • Quality
  • Action based on institutional ethos and values

OUR MOTTO

Virtute et Sapientia Duc Mundum

In Virtue and Wisdom, Lead the World.
